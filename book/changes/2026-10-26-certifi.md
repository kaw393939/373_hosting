vulnerable `certifi 2026.7.22`. Host updates cannot replace its locked Python
dependencies. Application [PR #46](https://github.com/kaw393939/is373-ai-chat/pull/46) owns the correction:

- [Owning issue #44](https://github.com/kaw393939/is373-ai-chat/issues/44) holds acceptance criteria.
- [Dependency requirement](https://github.com/kaw393939/is373-ai-chat/blob/main/docs/requirements.md#dependency-security-maintenance) defines the intended behavior.
- [Canonical certifi change record](https://github.com/kaw393939/is373-ai-chat/blob/main/docs/audit/2026-10-26-certifi.md) records the advisory, reviewed patch and actual evidence.

Compare three evidence types: scans check known findings; image-local TLS
fixtures test trusted/rejected connections; environment records establish which
digest runs. Positive/negative describe cases, separately from test levels.

Green CI alone proves neither a patched deployment nor zero vulnerabilities.
Preserve unrelated services and keep secrets outside Git.
